# Academy

The Academy is Enigma's structured learning system. It provides curriculum, lessons, concepts, exercises, research tasks, exams, and certifications to systematically build Enigma's capabilities.

## Philosophy

The Academy follows these principles:

1. **Structured Learning**: Learning follows a defined curriculum
2. **Evidence-Based**: All learning is based on evidence
3. **Governed Knowledge**: Academy knowledge passes through Governance
4. **Practical Application**: Learning includes practical exercises
5. **Certification**: Completion is validated through exams

## Academy Architecture

```
Academy Layer
  ↓
Curriculum
  ↓

  ↓

  ↓

  ↓

```

## Lessons

2. **Prerequisites**: Required prior knowledge
3. **Content**: The lesson material
4. **Examples**: Real-world examples

- **Project-Based**: Lessons that build projects

## Concepts

### Concept Definition

Concepts are the fundamental units of knowledge within lessons. Each concept has:

1. **Name**: Concept identifier
2. **Definition**: Clear definition
3. **Explanation**: Detailed explanation
4. **Examples**: Illustrative examples
5. **Related Concepts**: Connections to other concepts
6. **Prerequisites**: Required concepts
7. **Maturity Level**: Knowledge maturity (0-5)

### Concept Relationships

Concepts are connected through:

- **Prerequisite**: Concept A is required for Concept B
- **Related To**: Concept A is related to Concept B
- **Supports**: Concept A supports Concept B
- **Contradicts**: Concept A contradicts Concept B
- **Depends On**: Concept A depends on Concept B

### Concept Governance

All concepts must pass through Knowledge Governance:

1. **Validation**: Concept definition is validated
2. **Scoring**: Evidence quality is scored
3. **Conflict Detection**: Conflicts with existing concepts are detected
4. **Resolution**: Conflicts are resolved
5. **Versioning**: Concept versions are tracked
6. **Maturity**: Concept maturity is updated

## Knowledge Maturity Levels

### Level 0: Unknown

- **Definition**: No knowledge exists
- **Evidence**: No evidence
- **Capability**: Cannot perform
- **Action**: Requires learning

### Level 1: Definition

- **Definition**: Concept defined
- **Evidence**: At least 1 evidence
- **Capability**: Understands concept
- **Action**: Can explain concept

### Level 2: Multiple Sources

- **Definition**: Multiple sources support definition
- **Evidence**: At least 2 evidence, 2 source types
- **Capability**: Can discuss concept
- **Action**: Can apply concept with guidance

### Level 3: Applied

- **Definition**: Concept applied in practice
- **Evidence**: At least 2 high-quality evidence, 3 total
- **Capability**: Can apply concept independently
- **Action**: Can use concept in work

### Level 4: Validated

- **Definition**: Concept validated in real projects
- **Evidence**: At least 3 high-quality evidence, 5 total
- **Capability**: Can teach concept
- **Action**: Can guide others in concept

### Level 5: Expert Knowledge

- **Definition**: Concept mastered at expert level
- **Evidence**: At least 5 high-quality evidence, 3 source types
- **Capability**: Can innovate in concept
- **Action**: Can advance the field
