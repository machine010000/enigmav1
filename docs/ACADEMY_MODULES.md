# Academy Modules

## Purpose

The Academy is divided into modules so that knowledge can be organized, reused, and applied by the right reasoning context.

## Academy Structure

Academy

├── Marketing
│   ├── Buyer Psychology
│   ├── Organic
│   ├── Paid
│   ├── Branding
│   └── Copywriting
├── Business
├── Programming
├── Sales
├── Marketplace
├── Social Media
└── Analytics

## Module Definitions

### Marketing
- Purpose: Guide messaging, positioning, audience framing, and value communication.
- Topics: positioning, messaging, channels, segmentation, conversion narratives.
- Workers using it: research service, planner, master brain, content workers.
- Update frequency: after each major campaign or decision cycle.

### Buyer Psychology
- Purpose: Guide understanding of motivation, objections, trust, and decision barriers.
- Topics: needs, fears, incentives, friction, trust signals.
- Workers using it: research service, planning, decision engine.
- Update frequency: weekly or after repeated pattern observations.

### Organic
- Purpose: Guide long-term discoverability and audience growth without paid spend.
- Topics: content strategy, SEO, distribution, authority building.
- Workers using it: research, content planning, growth workers.
- Update frequency: monthly or after performance review.

### Paid
- Purpose: Guide spending efficiency, targeting, conversions, and acquisition strategy.
- Topics: ad positioning, bidding logic, conversion hooks, audience fit.
- Workers using it: campaign workers, analytics workers.
- Update frequency: after campaign review cycles.

### Branding
- Purpose: Guide identity, voice, consistency, and emotional positioning.
- Topics: tone, perception, brand promise, narrative cohesion.
- Workers using it: messaging workers, product strategy workers.
- Update frequency: quarterly or when brand shifts occur.

### Copywriting
- Purpose: Guide persuasive written communication for product, marketing, and sales.
- Topics: hooks, headlines, calls to action, offer framing.
- Workers using it: content workers, campaign workers.
- Update frequency: per launch or content cycle.

### Business
- Purpose: Guide business model, growth logic, revenue mechanics, and strategic trade-offs.
- Topics: monetization, growth levers, market fit, opportunity cost.
- Workers using it: master brain, planner, decision engine.
- Update frequency: after strategic reviews.

### Programming
- Purpose: Guide architecture, system boundaries, implementation patterns, and technical trade-offs.
- Topics: modularity, interfaces, scalability, design principles.
- Workers using it: engineering workers, planner, execution engine.
- Update frequency: per architecture milestone or release.

### Sales
- Purpose: Guide persuasion, objections, close strategy, and offer framing.
- Topics: objections, trust building, qualifying signals, conversion paths.
- Workers using it: sales workers, research workers.
- Update frequency: after sales cycle reviews.

### Marketplace
- Purpose: Guide ecosystem behavior, supply-demand patterns, and competitive dynamics.
- Topics: market structure, competition, trust, positioning, demand signals.
- Workers using it: research, planner, strategy workers.
- Update frequency: monthly or after market shifts.

### Social Media
- Purpose: Guide channel-specific strategy and audience engagement.
- Topics: platform behavior, content formats, hooks, engagement patterns.
- Workers using it: social workers, content workers.
- Update frequency: per campaign cycle.

### Analytics
- Purpose: Guide measurement, KPI interpretation, and feedback-based improvement.
- Topics: metrics, attribution, quality signals, evaluation frameworks.
- Workers using it: dashboard workers, decision engine, planner.
- Update frequency: continuously as data arrives.

## Module Rules

- Each module should contain reusable knowledge only.
- Each module should be linked to one or more workers where practical.
- Updates should be evidence-driven and versioned where possible.
